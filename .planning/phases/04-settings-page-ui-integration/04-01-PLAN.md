- Header: `<h2>Google Sheets 連接</h2>` inside a card.
- State: `gasUrl` and `sheetId` strings initialized from `SettingsService.getSheetsConfig()?.gasUrl ?? ""` and `?.sheetId ?? ""`. Plus `sheetsError: string | null` for validation error.
- Two text inputs:
  - GAS 網址: `<input type="text" placeholder="https://script.google.com/...">` with `value={gasUrl}`
  - Sheet ID: `<input type="text" placeholder="Sheet ID">` with `value={sheetId}`

- Success feedback: After successful save, briefly show `"已儲存"` in `text-emerald-400` (use a `useState<boolean>` that resets after 2 seconds via `setTimeout`).
